FGS CAPITAL & DEVELOPMENT — PUBLISHING

NETLIFY
1. Log in to Netlify.
2. Open Sites / Add new site / Deploy manually.
3. Drag the entire folder "fgs-capital-site" or the ZIP contents.
4. After deploy, open Domain management / Add a domain / Add fgscap.com.
5. Netlify will show the exact DNS records required for your site.

IONOS DNS
6. In IONOS, open Domains & SSL > fgscap.com > DNS.
7. Enter the DNS records exactly as Netlify shows. Do not change email MX records.
8. Add www.fgscap.com in Netlify as an alias if desired.
9. Wait for DNS/SSL verification; Netlify will issue HTTPS automatically.

IMPORTANT
- Do not delete or modify the IONOS MX records used by contact@fgscap.com and fernando@fgscap.com.
- The contact form is configured for Netlify Forms and will begin collecting submissions after deployment.
- The site contains a securities/investment disclaimer. Before stating that any fund is exchange-listed or publicly offered, add the verified legal vehicle name and applicable offering/listing information.
