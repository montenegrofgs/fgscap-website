FGS Capital Advisors is a Florida-based international capital advisory and real estate development platform focused on connecting investors, developers, landowners and strategic partners across global markets.

Fernando's background spans executive leadership in construction and real estate development, land acquisitions, commercial strategy, complex negotiations and the structuring of investment opportunities.

Our work centers on international capital raising, investment structuring, real estate development, strategic acquisitions and cross-border transactions, with the United States as a primary development market and selected international relationships supporting capital access and execution.

FGS Capital · Investment Strategies

FGS Aurion.
Knowledge is our gold.

FGS Capital is the institutional platform for origination, structuring and investment advisory. FGS Aurion is the proposed identity for distinct real estate investment strategies, each to be established and offered only under the appropriate legal and regulatory framework. Built by operators, for investors: disciplined underwriting, real assets and alignment of interests.

01 · Built for Yield

Aurion Yield

Income-producing real estate and selected opportunistic acquisitions in Brazil, the United States and, where feasible, Paraguay.

  • Operating assets, BTS and sale-leaseback, commercial properties and other contracted-income opportunities
  • Seek discounts to independently assessed value and a margin of safety
  • Underwrite tenant quality, cash flow, occupancy, maintenance, financing and currency exposure
  • Illustrative annual income objective of 10–12% in selected scenarios, not a guaranteed return
02 · Built for Development

Aurion Development

A land-focused development strategy designed to participate in project sales through financial land-swap agreements.

  • Acquire or control land subject to title, permits and full diligence
  • Structure a negotiated percentage of project sales through a financial permuta
  • Seek to avoid direct construction-capital exposure at the land vehicle level, subject to contractual design
  • Work with qualified developers and independent project controls
03 · Built for Credit

Aurion Credit

Real-estate-backed credit, receivables and structured financing, assessed transaction by transaction.

  • Collateral quality, enforceability and seniority
  • Conservative underwriting and borrower cash-flow analysis
  • Independent legal and asset due diligence
  • Clear covenants, monitoring and downside scenarios
04 · Built for Opportunity

Aurion Opportunities

Selective distressed assets, auctions and special situations in markets where FGS and its specialists can evaluate the underlying real estate.

  • Disciplined acquisition basis and legal review
  • Asset recovery and operational repositioning
  • Defined exit alternatives and downside analysis
  • No acquisition outside an approved investment thesis
FGS Capital is our institutional home. FGS Aurion is our investment signature. Knowledge is our true gold.

What We Do

U.S. Market Entry & Execution.

FGS leads business origination, land and project sourcing, negotiations, capital structuring and market-entry coordination. Technical execution services are provided by independently contracted specialists when a project advances.

01

U.S. Market Entry — 120-Day Soft Landing

An FGS-led, 120-day commercial and executive market-entry engagement centered on a concrete U.S. real estate opportunity: an FGS-sourced project or a client-defined acquisition and development mandate.

  • Investment objectives, target market and project selection
  • Land sourcing, acquisition negotiations and transaction coordination
  • Preliminary business case, capital strategy and investment structuring
  • Coordination with independently retained legal, tax, banking and licensed specialists
  • Joint project-development coordination with strategic business partner 4C, with technical scopes separately agreed
  • Continued FGS commercial involvement alongside the technical project team through agreed execution milestones

Land acquisition, construction capital and third-party professional services are separate from the FGS MVP fee. 4C is an independent strategic business partner for separately contracted technical services, not an automatic MVP co-provider. No staff training or technical knowledge-transfer commitment is included.

02

Development & Capital Structuring

Transforming an opportunity into an investment-ready business case.

  • Feasibility and underwriting
  • Capital stack, Sources & Uses and funding strategy
  • SPV, governance and investor-alignment interfaces
  • Waterfall, fee and risk review coordination
  • Investor materials, data-room organization and capital-partner dialogue
03

Owner’s Representation | Specialist Services

Independent technical oversight may be arranged through qualified third-party providers, including 4C, under separate contracts to protect the owner and investor perspective.

  • Milestone validation and executive investor reporting

Development Management & Project Controls
