About FGS

Strategic Experience.
Local Execution.

FGS Capital & Development is a Florida-based advisory platform led by Fernando Montenegro, a real estate and business executive with more than three decades of experience across Brazil, Angola and the United States.

His background spans residential development, land acquisition, feasibility, commercial strategy, investor relations, capital structuring, executive leadership and complex negotiations with developers, landowners, financial institutions and public stakeholders.

FGS was created to bring those disciplines together around one objective: make real estate opportunities more investable, governable and executable.

What We Do

Four Integrated Fronts.

FGS is intentionally focused. The initial platform is built around four complementary scopes that connect capital, governance, technical controls and execution — without building a heavy operating structure.

01

Development & Capital Structuring

Transforming an opportunity into an investment-ready business case.

  • Feasibility and underwriting
  • Capital stack, Sources & Uses and funding strategy
  • SPV, governance and investor-alignment interfaces
  • Waterfall, fee and risk review coordination
  • Investor materials, data-room organization and capital-partner dialogue
02

Independent Engineer & Owner’s Representation

Independent oversight focused on protecting the owner and investor perspective.

  • Validate reported progress against site reality
  • Budget, committed cost and actual-cost reporting
  • Schedule, risk and forecast-at-completion monitoring
  • Contract, documentation and lien-waiver checkpoints
  • Milestone validation and executive investor reporting
03

Development Advisory & Project Controls

Supporting the project from diligence and procurement through delivery.

  • Due-diligence coordination and RFI management
  • Bid / contractor procurement support
  • Budget benchmarking and cost-overrun early warning
  • Schedule and critical-path monitoring
  • Technical, commercial and execution-risk coordination
04

Sustainability & Performance

Integrating sustainability where it improves project performance, financing, marketability or resilience.

  • Green-building certification strategy
  • Energy-efficiency simulations
  • Decarbonization and climate-resilience advisory
  • Building-performance and ESG support
  • Coordination with specialized technical partners
