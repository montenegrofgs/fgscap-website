About FGS Capital Advisors

Connecting Global Capital
to Real Estate Opportunities.

FGS Capital is a Florida-based real estate investment, land-capital and structuring platform connecting landowners, developers and capital providers. FGS evaluates land acquisitions, financial land swaps and build-to-suit transactions rather than performing construction.

Founded and led by Fernando Lima, a real estate and business executive with more than 30 years of experience across Brazil, Angola and the United States, FGS brings together entrepreneurial leadership, international capital origination, investment structuring and disciplined real estate execution.

Fernando's background spans executive leadership in construction and real estate development, land acquisitions, commercial strategy, complex negotiations and the structuring of investment opportunities.

Our work centers on land investment and financing, financial land swaps, build-to-suit structures, investment analysis and cross-border real estate transactions, with the United States as a primary development market and selected international relationships supporting capital access and execution.

A DREAM · A CALLING · A FAMILY PURPOSE

A golden seed in someone else's life.

Golden Seed was born from a dream and God's calling to our family. It represents not wealth, but the precious knowledge of His Word and the power of faith to transform lives.

Our deepest purpose is to bring faith and hope to children and young people who have lost hope, often deprived of the love, security and opportunities every child deserves. We seek to support pastors, missionaries and causes that share the Gospel, restore dignity, rebuild family bonds and open pathways toward education, work and a new beginning.

Money is not our mission. It is simply a vehicle to sustain extraordinary missionaries who dedicate their lives and families to serving others. We hope to be a small golden seed in the lives they touch.

Our family dedicates a meaningful portion of the FGS founder's personal earnings to this calling. We believe all silver and gold belong to God, and every door opened through FGS is a blessing entrusted to our care. We need only enough to live in peace and security; our greater purpose is to serve others and return all honor and glory to Him.

01 · Our Purpose

To plant seeds of faith that outlive us.

Golden Seed Project is rooted in the Christian conviction that everything entrusted to us should be used responsibly, with gratitude and generosity. We seek to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, support those who dedicate their lives to missionary work, and help bring hope, nourishment, education and dignity to people and communities in need. Financial success is not the destination; it is one of the means through which we can serve.

02 · The Founding Family

A family legacy, intentionally nurtured.

Fernando Lima and his family conceived Golden Seed as a purpose to be lived and passed from generation to generation. From their daughters' earliest years, the family sought to teach faith in Jesus, prayer, evangelism, generosity, tithing, compassion and practical care for others.

Mathematics and financial literacy were part of that same education. Their daughters began Kumon at ages three and four, respectively, because their parents wanted them to understand numbers, discipline and the responsible stewardship of money—not merely to accumulate wealth, but to become capable of sustaining and multiplying acts of service over a lifetime.

For this family, the most meaningful inheritance is not only financial: it is a commitment to love God, help one's neighbor and use knowledge and resources wisely.

The Apple of God's Eyes (Menina dos Olhos de Deus) works to protect girls affected by trafficking and exploitation, providing a safer environment and support for rebuilding their lives through care, education and dignity.

According to the organization's published presentation shared with us, its mission has served children across several countries for more than 25 years. Its Nepal program traces its work to Kathmandu in 2000, and the Cambodia initiative to Siem Reap in 2007.

NEPALProtection, care and reintegration for girls affected by human trafficking
CAMBODIAProtection and rehabilitation for girls exposed to exploitation

These are the organization's own programs and reported milestones, not programs operated by FGS Capital. Photographic material is subject to publication rights and child-protection safeguards.

Education · Development · Family care

INAPE · Clínica Multidisciplinar Noitikós

Founded in 2011 by educator Maria Aparecida Sabará, INAPE – Clínica Multidisciplinar Noitikós grew from her classroom experience and the need for individualized support for children facing learning and developmental challenges.

The clinic's mission is to provide welcoming, human-centered and multidisciplinary care for children, adolescents and their families, respecting each person's individuality, potential and life story.

Since 2011Education, care and the opportunity to develop each person's potential.

Noitikós is an independent clinic. Its clinical services are distinct from Golden Seed's Christian missionary initiatives; no corporate ownership or formal financial sponsorship by FGS is implied.
