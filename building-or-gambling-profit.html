Are You Building or Gambling With Your Profit?

Experienced project controls are not overhead. They are profit protection.

There is an old saying in Brazil: “The owner’s eye fattens the cattle.”

After 30 years in real estate, including seven years as CEO of a development and construction company, I have seen developers save a few thousand dollars per month on professional project controls, only to lose multiples of that amount through delays, change orders, poor documentation and late cost-overrun detection.

Construction management is not simply checking invoices. It means continuously controlling:

  • budgeted, committed and actual costs;
  • cost-to-complete forecasts;
  • schedule and critical-path risks;
  • change orders and contract compliance;
  • payment applications and lien waivers;
  • quality, documentation and investor reporting.

Developers should dedicate their highest-value time to selecting the right land, structuring capital, defining the product, building the team and driving sales, not discovering cost surprises at the end of construction.

Experienced project controls are not overhead. They are profit protection.

Construction is not simply raising capital, hiring a contractor and waiting for completion. Buildings carry warranties, liabilities and long-term consequences.
