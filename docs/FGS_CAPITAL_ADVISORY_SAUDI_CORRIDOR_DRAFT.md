# FGS Capital Advisors — Positioning (Internal Draft)

## Core positioning
FGS is a cross-border **capital advisory** platform. Approximately **80% of its activities focus on real estate**, with complementary M&A, capital structuring, strategic transactions and international investment origination.

### Suggested English headline
**Global Capital Advisory. Real Estate at Our Core.**
Capital Structuring · Real Estate Development · M&A · Cross-Border Investments.

### Suggested Portuguese positioning
A FGS Capital Advisors é uma plataforma internacional de assessoria estratégica, estruturação de capital e desenvolvimento de negócios, com aproximadamente 80% de sua atuação concentrada no setor imobiliário. Conectamos capital, oportunidades e execução nos Estados Unidos, no Brasil e em Angola, com acesso à rede internacional de parceiros da 4C Americas.

## Angola–Saudi Arabia corridor
The user reports a relationship through the **Angola–Saudi Arabia Chamber of Commerce** with the aim of originating Saudi investor interest in U.S. investments, especially real estate. Describe this as an **institutional relationship / prospective capital origination channel**, not a signed funding mandate, an established investor commitment, a Saudi office or endorsement.

Suggested site subsection: **Africa–Middle East–U.S. Investment Corridor**.
Suggested copy: 'A FGS desenvolve relacionamentos estratégicos que conectam oportunidades de investimento nos Estados Unidos a empresários e investidores de Angola e da Arábia Saudita, incluindo interlocução por meio da Câmara de Comércio Angola–Arábia Saudita.'

## Verification before publishing
- Chamber's exact official legal name, nature of relationship and consent to name/logo.
- Any Saudi counterparties and their authorized public disclosure.
- No guaranteed fundraising or investment commitments.
- 80% figure is user-provided approximate business mix, not audited statistic.
- Regulatory review of investment marketing in all target jurisdictions.
