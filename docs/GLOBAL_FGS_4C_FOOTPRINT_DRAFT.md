**Geographic footprint:** Estados Unidos (Flórida e Califórnia) · Brasil · Angola · México · Argentina · Chile · Colômbia · Costa Rica.

## Presentation rules
- Do not describe all network offices as FGS-owned offices. Label as FGS + 4C network and verify the entity responsible for each location.
- Angola presence should be described according to documented FGS office/representation/project status; no 'only US fund in Angola' exclusivity claim without market verification.
- Verify specific addresses, legal entities and permission to use 4C logos before showing as formal office locations.
- No claim that institutional investors, banks or clients endorse FGS without authorization.
