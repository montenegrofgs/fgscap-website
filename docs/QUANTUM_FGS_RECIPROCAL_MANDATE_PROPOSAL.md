# FGS Capital Advisors × Quantum — Proposed Reciprocal Mandate

**Status: PROPOSAL ONLY — NOT SIGNED OR PUBLICLY ANNOUNCED**

## Purpose
Propose to Milton (Quantum) a reciprocal business development, origination and representation mandate between FGS Capital Advisors and Quantum, covering mutually agreed opportunities in capital advisory, real estate, M&A and cross-border investment.

## Core terms for discussion
1. Parties: verify exact registered entities and authorized signatories.
2. Territory: United States, Brazil, Angola and additional mutually agreed markets, including potential Saudi relationships only where authorized.
3. Scope: deal sourcing, introductions, preliminary qualification, structuring support, coordinated investor/developer presentations.
4. Reciprocal authority: neither party may bind the other, promise financing, solicit securities, or represent regulatory authorization without express written consent and applicable licenses.
5. Non-exclusivity as default; any exclusivity only per specific project/territory and agreed term.
6. Economics: success fees and/or retainers negotiated in writing per opportunity; comply with securities/broker-dealer and real-estate brokerage laws.
7. Confidentiality, non-circumvention, conflicts of interest, client ownership, compliance and termination.
8. Joint use of brand, logos and website announcements only after both parties sign and approve final wording.

## Website wording AFTER signing and approval
'FGS Capital Advisors and Quantum maintain a reciprocal strategic representation and origination relationship for selected cross-border opportunities, subject to the scope of each mandate.'

## Website wording BEFORE signing

Milton, bom dia! Quero propor que formalizemos uma parceria estratégica entre a FGS Capital Advisors e a Quantum, por meio de um mandato recíproco de representação e originação de negócios.
