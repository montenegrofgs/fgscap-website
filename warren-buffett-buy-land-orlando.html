Land Acquisition & Investment Structuring

Would Warren Buffett Buy Land in Orlando Today?

Land acquisition is the art of combining negotiation, timing and vision.

Over my 30-year real estate career, I have negotiated and acquired more than 50 sites across different markets and price ranges.

One of my most complex assignments involved assembling 23 separately owned properties into a single 5,000-square-meter development site in a highly valued location—a transaction worth approximately US$40 million.

Imagine coordinating 23 owners, each with different expectations, legal circumstances and financial needs, and bringing every property to closing as part of one development strategy. But finding and acquiring land is only the beginning.

Transforming a site into a credible and investable development opportunity requires:

  • zoning, title, environmental and technical due diligence;
  • product definition and market analysis;
  • financial feasibility and capital structuring;
  • risk identification and mitigation;
  • negotiation with owners, developers and professional partners;
  • coordinated execution, governance and investor reporting.

This takes time, experience and many hours at the negotiating table. The objective is not simply to sell a parcel, collect a commission and move on. It is to connect land, capital, technical expertise and execution in a structured business capable of creating and protecting value.

I am currently working with experienced development and technical partners on selected opportunities in Greater Orlando that are progressing through land control, feasibility, approvals and project structuring.

In addition, a private U.S. real estate investment vehicle has been structured to evaluate and potentially capitalize a carefully selected pipeline of development opportunities. The projects are selected based on location, development potential, technical and legal feasibility, projected economics and risk profile.

My specialty is helping investors, developers and construction companies transform real estate opportunities into structured, executable businesses.

Qualified investors, family offices, developers and strategic partners interested in learning more are welcome to contact me privately. Opportunities will be presented only through the applicable offering documents, eligibility requirements and regulatory framework. Every investment requires independent evaluation and due diligence.

Qualified accredited investors interested in Central Florida development opportunities, with anticipated commitments starting at US$100,000, are welcome to contact me privately for further information.
